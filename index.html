    What this list does and doesn't do

    This tool scopes which criteria apply to your tool. It does not certify conformance. Conformance still requires human testing — a keyboard walkthrough and a screen-reader pass — because automated scanners catch only about a third of issues, and the hardest parts of interactive data tools (focus management, meaningful alternatives, drag alternatives) are exactly the parts a scanner cannot judge.

    Open educational resource. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing you enter leaves this page. ★ marks criteria new in WCAG 2.2, where interactive data tools most often trip.
